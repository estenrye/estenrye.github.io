# Enabling IAM Identity Center

## References

- [AWS IAM Identity Center User Guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/singlesignon/latest/userguide/what-is.html)

## Overview

I've been using AWS Single Sign-On (SSO) for a while now, and I've been impressed with the ease of use and the security it provides.  I recently learned about the IAM Identity Center and wanted to enable it for my organization.  The IAM Identity Center provides a central location for managing your AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM) resources.  It allows you to view and manage your IAM users, groups, roles, and policies in one place.  This makes it easier to manage your IAM resources and ensure that your organization is following best practices for security and compliance.
